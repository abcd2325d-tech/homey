    How to design

    1. Add shapes from the left panel. If something is selected, the new shape is stacked on top of it.
    2. Draw shape: tap corners (or drag freely) on the ground to draw any outline, then press Finish. It becomes a solid you can make taller with Size Y.
    3. Spin shape: draw the right half of a side outline (like half a cup). It spins into a round object.
    4. Text: adds 3D letters. Change the words in Properties.
    5. Move, rotate, resize with the arrows on the selected shape, or type exact numbers in Properties.
    6. Make holes: set a shape to Hole, place it inside another shape, select both and press Combine to cut it out. Align centers puts the hole exactly in the middle.
    7. Combine also glues solid shapes together into one object.
    8. Export STL to 3D print (1 grid square = 10 mm), or GLB/OBJ for other apps.
