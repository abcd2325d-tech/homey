    How to design

    1. Add shapes from the left panel. They appear where the camera is looking.
    2. Move, rotate, resize with the arrows on the selected shape, or type exact numbers in Properties.
    3. Make holes: set a shape to Hole, place it inside another shape, select both and press Combine to cut it out.
    4. Combine also glues solid shapes together into one object.
    5. Export STL to 3D print (1 grid square = 10 mm), or GLB/OBJ for other apps.
